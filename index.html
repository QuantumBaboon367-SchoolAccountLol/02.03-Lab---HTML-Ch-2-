Meridan, Idaho

Location

Meridian is a city located in Ada County, Idaho. Meridian borders / flows into the city of Boise, the state of Idaho's capitol.

About

Weather

The weather in Meridian can be pretty varied, as for the most part each season is pretty distinct. That being said, certain weather patterns can linger now and again, summer heat extending into fall, snow into spring, etc.

Population

Currently somewhere in around 148,000 people live in Meridian, ID. This makes Meridian Idaho's second largest city!

Cool Places

I dunno anything about local sports, but I do know about where to go for (what I would call, anyway) trinkets and treasures. For books and multimedia collectors, there's a newly constructed Barnes & Noble location on Eagle Road as well as Half Priced Books (my favorite) on Eagle and Fairview.
